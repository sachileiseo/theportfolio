Why the content brief check matters most

Most delays and revisions come from a brief that skipped one of these. Catching it before writing starts saves both sides a rewrite.

01

No outline, no draft

A brief without an H1-H4 structure means the writer is guessing at what the client actually wants covered. I ask for the outline first, or build one and get it approved before writing a word.

02

Cannibalization check first

Before a new page gets written, I check it against the client's existing pages. Two pages competing for the same keyword hurts both, better to catch it at the brief stage than after publishing.

03

Voice rules travel with the brief

UK English and first-person plural for one client, action-first CTAs for another. The voice guide isn't optional context, it's part of the brief itself.
